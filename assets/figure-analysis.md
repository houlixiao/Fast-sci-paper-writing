# Figure-to-text analysis worksheet

> Copy one block per figure, table or multi-panel result. Do not estimate missing numeric data from unreadable graphics.

- **Figure/Table identifier:** [Fig. / Table ...]
- **Research question answered:** [What claim is this evidence for?]
- **Object, variables, conditions:** [Axis quantities, units, groups, time, physical assumptions]
- **Directly observed fact:** [Quantified trend or difference; no causal interpretation]
- **Comparison / uncertainty:** [Baseline, replicate/uncertainty if available]
- **Mechanistic interpretation:** [Physical reasoning supported by equations, decompositions or references]
- **Competing explanation or limitation:** [Where interpretation is not unique or extrapolation is unsafe]
- **Manuscript location:** [Section name]
- **Reference(s) needed:** [Real verified sources, if applicable]

## Compact paragraph blueprint

`Figure [n] presents [observable response] under [conditions]. [One or two quantitative observations]. [Mechanistic explanation with evidence or appropriately cautious wording]. [Implication for the paper's specific research question].`
