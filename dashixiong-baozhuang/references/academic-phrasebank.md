# 学术短语库

按论文章节组织的高频学术句式。在润色和改写时参考使用。

---

## Abstract / 摘要

### 背景引入
- `[Topic] has emerged as a critical challenge in [field].`
- `The ability to [capability] is essential for [application].`
- `Despite decades of research, [problem] remains poorly understood.`

### 问题/不足
- `However, existing approaches suffer from [limitation].`
- `Current methods are limited by [constraint], making them unsuitable for [scenario].`
- `A key unresolved question is [question].`

### 本文工作
- `Here we present a novel [method/model/approach] to [objective].`
- `In this work, we propose [approach] that enables [capability].`
- `We demonstrate a [technique] that overcomes [long-standing limitation].`

### 核心发现
- `We find that [key finding], revealing [insight].`
- `Our results show a [X%] improvement in [metric] compared to [baseline].`
- `We identify [mechanism] as the primary driver of [phenomenon].`

### 意义
- `These findings provide new insights into [field/mechanism].`
- `This work establishes a framework for [broader application].`
- `The proposed approach opens new possibilities for [application area].`

---

## Introduction / 引言

### 研究背景
- `[Field/Technology] plays a pivotal role in [broader context].`
- `The growing demand for [application] has motivated extensive research on [topic].`
- `[Phenomenon] has attracted considerable attention due to its importance in [field].`

### 文献综述过渡
- `Extensive efforts have been devoted to addressing [problem].`
- `The literature on [topic] can be broadly categorized into [X] approaches.`
- `Previous studies have primarily focused on [aspect A] and [aspect B].`

### 现有研究不足（核心段落）
- `However, several critical gaps remain.`
- `First, [approach A] assumes [simplification], which may not hold under [realistic condition].`
- `Second, existing studies have largely overlooked [important factor].`
- `Third, limited attention has been paid to [neglected aspect], despite its demonstrated impact on [outcome].`
- `Consequently, a comprehensive framework that accounts for [factors] is still lacking.`

### 本文贡献
- `To address these gaps, this study presents [approach].`
- `The key contributions of this work are threefold:`
- `First, we [contribution 1]. Second, we [contribution 2]. Third, we [contribution 3].`
- `Unlike previous approaches that [limitation], our method [advantage].`
- `To the best of our knowledge, this is the first study to [novel aspect].`

## Methods / 方法

### 整体描述
- `A schematic overview of the [experimental setup / numerical framework] is shown in Figure X.`
- `The [method] consists of [N] main components: [list].`
- `The workflow of the proposed approach is as follows: [step 1 → step 2 → step 3].`

### 参数说明
- `The parameter [name] represents [physical meaning] and was determined by [method].`
- `[Parameter] was set to [value], based on [justification / reference].`
- `The value of [parameter] was obtained through [calibration procedure].`
- `A sensitivity analysis confirmed that the results are robust to variations in [parameter] within [range].`

### 干扰排除
- `To eliminate boundary effects, [measure] was implemented.`
- `[Factor] was controlled by [procedure] to isolate the effect of [variable of interest].`
- `A mesh independence study was conducted (see Supplementary Material) to ensure that the results are insensitive to further mesh refinement.`
- `Each experiment was repeated [N] times to ensure reproducibility.`

### 验证
- `The model was validated against [benchmark data] from [source].`
- `Good agreement was observed between the numerical predictions and experimental measurements (R² = [value], Figure X).`
- `The accuracy of the proposed method was assessed by comparison with [alternative methods].`

---

## Results / 结果

### 数据呈现
- `Figure X presents [what is shown] under [conditions].`
- `As [variable] increases from [A] to [B], [response] [increases/decreases/exhibits a nonlinear trend].`
- `A [X%] increase in [metric] was observed when [condition].`

### 规律分析
- `Notably, [response] reaches a maximum at [value], beyond which it [declines/plateaus].`
- `The data reveal a [linear/logarithmic/power-law] relationship between [X] and [Y].`
- `This trend is consistent across all [conditions/groups/samples] tested.`

### 机理分析
- `This behavior can be attributed to [mechanism], whereby [detailed explanation].`
- `The observed [phenomenon] arises from the interplay between [factor A] and [factor B].`
- `As [condition changes], [mechanism] becomes dominant, leading to [observed effect].`
- `The enhancement in [property] is primarily driven by [underlying cause].`

### 异常数据
- `Interestingly, a deviation from the expected trend was observed at [condition].`
- `This anomaly may be explained by [possible reason], which is consistent with [evidence/support].`
- `Further investigation is needed to fully elucidate the origin of this effect.`

### 对比
- `Compared with [existing method], the proposed approach achieves a [X%] improvement in [metric].`
- `The results are in good agreement with those reported by [citation] for similar [systems/conditions].`
- `The key advantage of our method over [alternative] is [specific advantage].`

---

## Discussion / 讨论

### 结果解读
- `These findings suggest that [broader implication].`
- `Taken together, the results indicate that [mechanism/process] plays a central role in [phenomenon].`
- `The [observed effect] can be understood in the context of [theory/framework].`

### 与文献对比
- `Our findings are consistent with [citation], who reported [similar finding] in [context].`
- `In contrast to previous studies that [finding], our results show [difference], which may be due to [reason].`
- `The discrepancy between our results and those of [citation] likely stems from [methodological difference].`

### 局限性
- `Several limitations of this study should be acknowledged.`
- `First, [limitation]. Future work should [how to address it].`
- `The present analysis is limited to [scope]; extension to [broader context] would be valuable.`
- `While the results are promising, further validation under [condition] is warranted.`

---

## Conclusion / 结论

### 总结工作
- `In summary, this study has [achieved/demonstrated/revealed] [main contribution].`
- `We have presented a [novel/robust/comprehensive] [method/framework/approach] for [objective].`
- `The key findings are: (1) [finding 1]; (2) [finding 2]; (3) [finding 3].`

### 创新意义
- `This work represents a significant step toward [goal].`
- `The proposed approach provides a new paradigm for [application area].`
- `These insights open new avenues for [future research direction].`

### 展望
- `Future work will focus on [extension/improvement].`
- `Further studies are needed to [validate/extend/apply] these findings in [context].`
- `The methodology developed here can be extended to [related problems/systems].`

## 常用连接与过渡

### 因果关系
- `This is because / owing to / due to / as a result of / attributed to`
- `Consequently, / As a consequence, / Hence, / Therefore, / Thus,`

### 对比转折
- `However, / Nevertheless, / Nonetheless, / In contrast, / On the other hand,`
- `Although / While / Whereas / Despite / In spite of`

### 递进并列
- `Furthermore, / Moreover, / In addition, / Additionally,`
- `Similarly, / Likewise, / Correspondingly,`

### 强调
- `Notably, / Importantly, / It is worth noting that / In particular, / Specifically,`

### 总结
- `Overall, / Taken together, / In summary, / Collectively, these results demonstrate that`

## 常见中式英语修正

| 中式表达 | 推荐替换 |
|---------|---------|
| `a lot of` | `many / numerous / considerable / substantial` |
| `big / great` | `large / significant / major / pronounced` |
| `get / got` | `obtain / achieve / acquire / attain` |
| `find out` | `determine / identify / discover / reveal` |
| `make sure` | `ensure / verify / confirm` |
| `try to` | `attempt to / seek to / aim to` |
| `very important` | `critical / essential / crucial / vital / fundamental` |
| `more and more` | `increasingly / progressively` |
| `on the one hand... on the other hand` | `While... / Although... / [单独使用，不要机械对译]` |
| `plays an important role` | `plays a critical/pivotal/central/key role` [或用更具体的描述替代] |
| `has been widely studied` | `has attracted considerable research attention` 或引用具体文献 |
| `obvious / obviously` | `evident / apparent / clear / notably` |
| `prove` (当指"表明"时) | `demonstrate / indicate / show / suggest / confirm` |
